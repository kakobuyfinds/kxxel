02 / Compare fit

Measure something you already wear

Use centimeters and match the seller’s measuring method. Record the reference measurement beside the listed size so you can compare options later.

ItemWhat to compare
SneakersMeasure a removable insole from heel to toe and note its widest point. Compare with the same measurements for the listed pair.
TopsLay a favorite piece flat. Measure pit to pit and shoulder seam to hem. Check whether the seller lists flat width or full circumference.
BottomsMeasure the waistband flat without stretching it, then record the front rise and inseam.
AccessoriesCompare your wrist circumference with the bracelet’s stated fit range. Check cap size with the strap adjusted to its midpoint.

04 / Field notes

Three checks before you order

Product links

How to check JoyaGoo product links

Open the live listing and match the seller, marketplace item ID, model, color and size to your shortlist. A Taobao, Weidian or 1688 link may lead to a page with multiple variants, so confirm which one is selected.

If an old JoyaBuy link stops working, look for the original listing ID. If you find a replacement listing, save it as a new entry and recheck its measurements and photos.

Quality control

How to read JoyaGoo QC photos

Quality-control (QC) photos can help you inspect the visible condition of the item photographed. Check whether the photos are of your warehouse item, a seller sample or a previous order.

For shoes, request both sides, the outsole and an insole measurement if those views are missing. For clothing, compare the label, seam finish and measured width. Ask about the return window while the item is still at the warehouse.

Delivered cost

How to make a JoyaGoo shipping estimate

Record the item cost, domestic delivery, any service fees and the international quote. Ask for the packed dimensions and compare actual weight with the route’s billable weight.

For a US, UK or EU delivery, enter the exact destination and postcode before comparing quotes. Confirm the currency and check whether taxes, duties or handling fees are collected separately.

05 / Quick answers

JoyaGoo spreadsheet FAQ

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

A JoyaGoo spreadsheet is a community shopping index for finding and comparing items before using a purchasing agent. This guide links to HipobuyFind category pages for browsing.

What can I do if a JoyaBuy or JoyaGoo link is broken?

Look for the original marketplace item ID and search for that ID. If you find a live page, verify the seller and variant before relying on the old row.

Can QC photos verify authenticity?

QC photos can show visible condition, color, construction and measurements. Use documented provenance or an independent authentication service when authenticity matters.

How do I choose between two sneaker sizes?

Compare the listing’s size chart and a measured insole with the insole of a pair that fits you. Check width and toe room too, especially when changing models.

How can I compare shipping quotes fairly?

Use the same destination, packed dimensions and weight for each quote. Compare the billable weight and included services, and check which charges are collected separately.
